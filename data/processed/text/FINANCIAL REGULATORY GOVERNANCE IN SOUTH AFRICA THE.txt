The model, which was first proposed by Michael Taylor in a pamphlet published by the Centre for the Study of Financial Innovation in 1994,5 was aimed, primarily, at the Bank of England. The UK <T>Prior to the advent of Twin Peaks, the UK’s different overseers for conduct and systemic issues in the financial sector were so numerous that it was described as an ‘alphabet soup’6 of regulators.
